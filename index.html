How to read NFLPropSignal V3

Status

BETS1 Highlight (Signal 80+) and the official V3 value/price gate both pass.
LEANPositive V3 value at the captured price, below the full BET conditions.
PARLAY LEGLower Risk: an easier alternate line meant as a parlay leg, not a standalone bet.
RESEARCHLongshot Plus (J1). Shown for forward study only; never a production BET or LEAN.
WATCHInteresting, but waiting on price, status, data or context.
NO BETEvaluated and visible; doesn't pass the production rules.
NOT MODELEDNo projection for this player/market, so V3 can't estimate it.

The numbers

Est. hitThe one official V3 probability: 25% raw model + 75% market.
Signal0–100 ranking score (S1 Straights, L1 Lower Risk, J1 Longshot). Not a probability. A gold star means S1 Highlight (80+).
V3 EVExpected value at the captured sportsbook price.
Bet toWorst American odds that still qualify: BET (+2% EV) and LEAN (0% EV). BET also needs Signal 80+ and every other gate.
RanksGame, slate window, and a provisional week rank. Week rank includes preferred lines that aren't actionable, so actionable cards can skip numbers.

Pages

  • Picks: what V3 recommends now, in weekly Signal order.
  • Games: everything V3 preferred and evaluated in one matchup, with the book spread, total and team totals.
  • Parlay: add priced lines from any player view (Shop this line) and the slip shows combined odds, V3's parlay hit chance and estimated EV. Price checks only, not picks.
  • Player view: tap a card (or Player) for that player's results against the main line each week, this week's floor and ceiling, the matchup and his role. Weeks 1–3 come from the V2 ledger.
  • All props: the full ledger, including rejected lines. Tap a row for the reasoning and other lines.
  • Performance: graded results of calls locked before kickoff, plus the deeper diagnostics.

Locked games

After kickoff, the last successful pre-kickoff state of a game is frozen and never rewritten. Pregame games refresh on each successful build.
